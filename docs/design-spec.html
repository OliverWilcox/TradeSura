Foundation

Design system

Trade-grade, not startup-cute. The visual language borrows from the engineer's own world: flue-iron chrome, a healthy burner-flame blue for actions and status, and copper — the metal that carries the money through the walls — reserved exclusively for income. If it's copper, it's cash.

Rules of the system

  • Copper = income, always and only.
  • Blue flame dot = a live, healthy state.
  • Money is always mono, always annualised somewhere on screen.
  • One primary action per screen, full-width, bottom.

Build priority key

P0 — this week P1 — weeks 2–3 P2 — post-launch

P0 is a complete sellable loop: verify → build plan → doorstep sale → Direct Debit live → dashboard shows the money.

SCREEN 03

Plan builder

P0
Purpose
Create up to three tiers from a service picker. Ships with pre-built templates (Essential ~£9 / Plus ~£15 / Complete ~£22) so an engineer can be live in two taps — or customise per service, per price.
Key logic
  • Service picker draws from a curated library; each service carries its own solicitor-approved clause that assembles into the plan document automatically.
  • Prohibited promises (free breakdown repair, guaranteed fix) are visibly BLOCKED with a one-line why — compliance as a feature, not a lecture.
  • Live take-home calculator shows his cut next to the Planlock fee — total fee transparency kills the #1 objection before it forms.
  • Suggested price ranges per tier from platform benchmarks (later: per-postcode).

SCREEN 04

Doorstep flow — the product

P0
Purpose
The single most important screen in the company. At the end of a service, the engineer taps “Offer a plan”, texts a link (or shows a QR), and watches the customer sign live — mandate done before he's packed the van.
Key logic
  • Link opens the white-label signup (Screen 09) pre-filled with customer name, address and appliance from the job record — the customer only picks a tier and enters bank details.
  • Live status via websocket: sent → opened → tier chosen → mandate → ACTIVE, with a full-screen “flame lit” celebration on completion.
  • If not completed in 15 minutes, automatic follow-up SMS that evening + 3 days later (“Your quote for Smith Heating's care plan is still open”).
  • Income-delta preview personalises the stakes before the customer even answers.

SCREEN 08

Messages & tickets

P1
Purpose
Plan customers report issues and message the engineer inside the plan — keeping the relationship (and the renewal) inside Planlock instead of a lost text thread.
Key logic — including the non-negotiable
  • Gas-emergency interlock: any customer message or ticket matching gas-smell / CO-alarm patterns triggers a full-screen script — “Do not use switches. Open windows. Call the National Gas Emergency line 0800 111 999 now.” — before the ticket can proceed. This ships in P0 wherever customers can type.
  • Tickets auto-tag by type and by plan coverage; priority ordering surfaces no-heat cases first.
  • Every resolved ticket ends with a one-tap review request (feeds his Google profile — retention through gratitude).

SCREEN 09

Customer signup (white-label web)

P0
Purpose
The 90 seconds that create the annuity. Opens from the doorstep text/QR pre-filled with name, address and boiler; the customer picks a tier and completes the GoCardless mandate in the embedded flow. No account, no password, no app.
Key logic
  • Middle tier visually anchored as “Most chosen” — classic three-tier anchoring lifts average revenue per plan.
  • Gas Safe verified badge + engineer's face/brand = the conversion; Planlock stays in the footer.
  • Total price shown before the mandate — DMCC-clean, no drip.
  • Cooling-off, cancel-anytime and the Direct Debit Guarantee stated up front: fairness as a closer, and legally required anyway.
  • Abandon → automatic evening + day-3 SMS nudges (from Screen 04 logic).

SCREEN 11

Cancel & save flow

P1
Purpose
Churn is where plan businesses die. The save flow offers pause and downgrade before cancel, and reminds the customer what they're about to lose — then, if they still want out, cancelling is genuinely two taps.
Key logic
  • Save offers: pause (3 months, keeps price), downgrade, and — engineer-configurable — one retention perk (e.g. 1 month free) usable once per customer.
  • Loss framing is factual, never dark-pattern: due-service value stated plainly, exit path always visible.
  • Two-tap cancellation with email confirmation — DMCC subscription rules built in, and “no hoops” becomes a selling point on Screen 09.
  • Cancelled customers enter an automated win-back at 6 and 11 months (service-due season).

Non-negotiables in v1 (compliance)

  • Plan wording locked to maintenance-and-priority clauses; breakdown-repair promises hard-blocked (insurance line).
  • Total monthly price visible before mandate; no fee revealed late.
  • 14-day cooling-off + two-tap cancellation + email confirmations.
  • Gas-emergency interlock (0800 111 999) wherever a customer can type.
  • SMS: sender = trading name, STOP handling, quiet hours.
  • Solicitor review of the template clauses before first real customer.

Metrics wired from day one

  • Activation: % of signed-up engineers selling plan #1 within 30/90 days.
  • Doorstep conversion: links sent → ACTIVE, and median completion time.
  • Plans per engineer at day 30/90; average tier price.
  • Collection health: failure %, recovery %, involuntary churn.
  • Voluntary churn + save-flow acceptance (pause/downgrade).
  • Campaign take-up per preset per season.
