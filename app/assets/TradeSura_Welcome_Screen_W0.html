1. Purpose & rules

  • Shown once. First launch only, and to signed-out users. A signed-in engineer never sees it — the app opens straight to his dashboard. A signed-out returning engineer sees it with "I already have an account" promoted (see states).
  • One message. Money, in the engineer's words. No feature list, no carousel of three swipeable slides — trade users skip those and it delays the tap.
  • One primary action. Start now. Everything else is quieter.
  • Language law applies: never insurance, cover, protection, warranty, doorstep. "Care plans", "at the end of the job", "you keep 80%".
  • Five-second test: a gas engineer standing at a merchant counter should understand what it is and what it pays before the kettle boils.

4. Motion & feel

  • Background image scales 1.00 → 1.03 over 8 seconds on a loop (almost imperceptible — makes the screen feel alive without distracting).
  • Brand block, headline, proof strip, buttons fade-and-rise in sequence, 80ms apart, total under 500ms. No splash screen before this — the app icon → this screen directly; the brand block is the splash.
  • Respect reduced-motion settings: all animation off.
  • Haptic on Start now (light impact). Small thing; engineers notice quality.

5. Telemetry

welcome_viewed (source: organic / claim / referral) · welcome_start_tapped · welcome_signin_tapped · welcome_dismissed (app closed on this screen) — the ratio of start-tapped to viewed is the screen's only score. Target ≥ 60% on organic, ≥ 85% on claim-link arrivals.

6. Copy alternatives (A/B later, not now)

  • B — question: What would 50 customers at £12 a month do for you? / sub: £5,760 a year, collected for you. Free to set up.
  • C — trade voice: Fit the boiler. Sell the plan. Get paid every month. / sub unchanged.
  • Ship A (mocked). Test B/C only once organic installs are meaningful — letters, not the welcome screen, decide the launch numbers.

7. Accept when

  1. Fresh install → this screen in under 1.5s with no separate splash
  2. Signed-in user never sees it; signed-out returning user sees Sign in promoted
  3. Claim and referral links personalise the screen and carry attribution through to the account record
  4. Both buttons ≥ 44px tall; primary ≥ 56px; type legible over the photo on the darkest and lightest crops
  5. No banned words anywhere on the screen
