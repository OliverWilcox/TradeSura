TradeSura — Screen Designs v2 · Full Dark

Every launch screen re-skinned to the W0 welcome language: navy depth, white primary actions, money in green, frosted chips, real hierarchy. Logic and flows unchanged from Build Spec v1 — this is the styling layer Oliver applies over what he's built.

Design system v2 — tokens

Surfaces

bg #0B1A2E — app background   bg-deep #081321 — nav bar

surface #122540 — cards   surface-2 #193150 — inputs, tiles

hero gradient #1A3A66 → #122540 — money card, customer page header, celebration moments

Lines: white at 10% (cards) and 18% (emphasis). Radius 16px cards, 14px buttons, 12px inputs.

Colour roles

money #3DD68C — pounds only. Never decorative.

blue #5FA3F0 — brand, active nav, links, OFFERED state

amber #F2B84B — attention: payment retrying, service overdue, missed money

red #FF6B6B — failures only

text #F3F7FB   muted #9FB6D0   dim #6E86A3

Buttons

  • Primary = white on navy with a soft shadow — the signature. One per screen.
  • Green reserved for the customer's "Join" and the engineer's WhatsApp share.
  • Ghost white 1.5px outline for secondary actions.
  • Disabled = 40% opacity, never a different colour.
  • Nav fab is white — the "offer" action is the brightest thing on every screen.

Type & depth

  • System stack. Headlines 800 weight, −1% tracking. Money 800, tabular numerals, one size class up.
  • Labels: 10.5px, 800, +12% tracking, uppercase, muted.
  • Depth via layered navy gradients and 30–45% black shadows — no pure black, no flat grey.
  • Frosted chips (white 6% + 10% line) for proof strips and status.

Motion & feel

  • Screen transitions: fade + 8px rise, 220ms.
  • Money figures count up on first appearance (400ms).
  • Haptics: light on primary taps, success pattern on plan live / payout landed.
  • Reduced-motion respected: all off.

Legibility rules (vans, daylight)

  • Body text never below #9FB6D0 on #122540 (≥ 6:1). Labels may use dim only at ≥ 10.5px 800.
  • Inputs one step lighter than cards so fields read as fields.
  • Status pills always carry text, never colour alone.
  • Auto-brightness hint: the app requests "prefer dark" status bar; no light-mode variant in October.
